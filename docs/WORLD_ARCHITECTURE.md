# EVShare 3D – PURE 3D WORLD ARCHITECTURE SPECIFICATION

## 1. The Spatial Metaverse Concept

In EVShare 3D, the application is not divided into HTML URLs or tabs. It is structured as a contiguous **EV Metaverse Complex** comprised of dedicated functional sectors, thematic rooms, and interactive spatial nodes.

```text
                                [ADMIN COMMAND CENTER]
                                          ▲
                                          │ Teleport Portal
                                          ▼
[OPERATIONS CENTER] ◄──► [EV CENTRAL GARAGE] ◄──► [AI INTELLIGENCE CENTER]
   │ (Staff Gate)             ▲         ▲                │
   ▼                          │         │                ▼
[SERVICE WORKSHOP]            │         │         [DECISION CHAMBER]
   │                          ▼         ▼
[DISPUTE ROOM]         [BOOKING CHAMBER] [CO-OWNERSHIP HALL]
                              │                 ▲

                       [FINANCE CENTER] ◄───────┤

                              ▼                 ▼
```

## 2. Sector & Room Specifications

### 2.1. Security Checkpoint (Login & Gateway)
* **Visual Theme**: High-tech entry security gate with atmospheric volumetric fog and cyan perimeter laser barriers.
* **Core 3D Objects**:
  * **Holographic Identity Pedestal**: Floating biometric console activating when the user approaches within 3 units.
  * **3D Virtual Keyboard**: Curved spatial keyboard hovering in 3D space with tactile physical key-press depression and acoustic feedback.
  * **Gateway Portal**: Massive cyber-industrial blast doors with hydraulic animations and light dispersal on successful authentication.

### 2.2. EV Central Garage (The Hub)
* **Visual Theme**: Sleek, futuristic architectural showroom with polished reflective flooring, ambient skylights, and active charging stalls.
* **Core 3D Objects**:
  * **Digital Twin Electric Vehicles**: Detailed 3D vehicle models parked in designated bays (`BAY-01`, `BAY-02`).
  * **Dynamic Equity Rings**: Glowing concentric 3D segmented toruses floating horizontally beneath each vehicle representing co-owner share % (e.g. 40% / 30% / 30%).
  * **Vehicle Status Beacon**: Overhead holographic sign displaying live battery SoC, range, and status (`AVAILABLE`, `BOOKED`, `CHARGING`).
  * **Teleportation Waypoints**: Luminous floor portals linking to specialized sector chambers.

### 2.3. Co-Ownership Hall
* **Visual Theme**: Cylindrical amphitheater with dark obsidian finishes and golden neon accents.
* **Core 3D Objects**:
  * **Member Identity Avatars**: 3D spatial pedestals for each co-owner displaying real-time ownership certificate, member badge, and trust tier.
  * **Syndicate Overview Pillar**: Central rotating cylinder displaying total mileage logged, cost efficiency, and legal registration status.

### 2.4. Booking Chamber
* **Visual Theme**: Chrono-spatial chamber surrounded by a sweeping panoramic calendar aura.
* **Core 3D Objects**:
  * **Spatial Timeline Cylinder / Arch**: A 3D curved timeline with day and hour blocks. Available slots glow calm blue; booked slots glow solid amber with the reserver's avatar glyph; maintenance slots glow hazard red.
  * **Time Range Selector Pegs**: Interactive draggable 3D handles allowing users to stretch the start and end of their booking.
  * **3D Reservation Confirmation Terminal**: Summarizes fairness impact, estimated costs, and features a physical "Confirm Booking" lock switch.

### 2.5. Energy & Finance Center
* **Visual Theme**: Clean, high-tech financial laboratory with emerald and cyan data pipelines.
* **Core 3D Objects**:
  * **Floating Expense Cubes**: 3D geometric crystals representing recent expenditures (Electricity, Maintenance, Insurance). Selecting a cube splits it into volumetric slices reflecting the cost allocation among co-owners.
  * **Cost Allocation Balances**: 3D bar graphs emerging from the floor comparing monthly obligations.
  * **3D Payment Kiosk**: Physical terminal with a contactless payment slot, simulated card tap pad, and QR scanner.

### 2.6. Shared Fund Vault
* **Visual Theme**: Cybernetic bank vault with heavy titanium locking mechanisms and reinforced glass energy cylinders.
* **Core 3D Objects**:
  * **Liquid Balance Column**: A transparent 3D cylindrical pillar whose glowing liquid level directly correlates with the fund's liquid VND balance against the safety reserve line.
  * **Transaction History Ribbon**: A continuous 3D scrolling holographic ribbon showing deposits and payouts with interactive nodes to expand receipts.

### 2.7. Digital Contract Room
* **Visual Theme**: Formal executive suite with floating holographic parchment.
* **Core 3D Objects**:
  * **3D Holographic Document**: Large, interactive floating manuscript supporting page-turning gestures, zoom, and spatial inspection of legal terms.
  * **Biometric Signature Pad**: Physical 3D stylus or touch plate that records the user's signature gesture, generating a visual seal that bonds to the contract.

### 2.8. Decision Chamber (Voting)
* **Visual Theme**: Futuristic parliamentary arena with a central holographic debate dais.
* **Core 3D Objects**:
  * **Proposal Hologram Pod**: Central display rendering proposed vehicle upgrades (e.g. battery pack replacement 3D exploded view).
  * **Voting Pedestals**: Interactive 3D biometric voting buttons (`APPROVE` - Emerald, `REJECT` - Ruby, `ABSTAIN` - Topaz).
  * **Live Quorum Cylinder**: Dynamic fluid column tracking total participating equity percentage against the 60% requirement.

### 2.9. AI Mobility Intelligence Center
* **Visual Theme**: Neural nexus with pulsating fiber-optic networks and deep indigo ambient lighting.
* **Core 3D Objects**:
  * **AI Core**: Concentric gyroscopic rings spinning around a pulsating central neural sphere.
  * **Recommendation Nodes**: Floating holographic data tablets presenting predictive battery health assessments, maintenance schedules, and fairness balance suggestions.

### 2.10. Operations Center (Staff)
* **Visual Theme**: Industrial control hangar with heavy diagnostic equipment.
* **Core 3D Objects**:
  * **3D QR Scanner Station**: Optical scanner station that projects laser scan lines when a co-owner presents their spatial QR badge.
  * **Telemetry Diagnostic Bench**: Digital twin diagnostic rig pulling instantaneous OBD-II readings and battery cell voltages.

### 2.11. Service Workshop (Maintenance)
* **Visual Theme**: Automotive service bay with hydraulic vehicle lift and robotic arms.
* **Core 3D Objects**:
  * **Hydraulic Vehicle Lift**: Allows raising the 3D vehicle to inspect undercarriage battery pack and suspension.
  * **Part Replacement Station**: Interactive tool rack enabling staff to simulate tire replacements, battery servicing, and cleaning.

### 2.12. Dispute Room
* **Visual Theme**: Neutral deliberation chamber with dual presentation consoles.
* **Core 3D Objects**:
  * **Dispute Crystal**: Crimson floating polyhedron containing the dispute dossier.
  * **Evidence Holotank**: 3D projection table displaying photos, 3D damage pinpoints, and trip logs submitted by parties.

### 2.13. Admin Command Center
* **Visual Theme**: Orbital observation deck overlooking the entire virtual metaverse.
* **Core 3D Objects**:
  * **The 7 Command Cores**: Interactive monolithic towers (User Core, Vehicle Core, Ownership Core, Booking Core, Finance Core, Dispute Core, System Core). Clicking a core expands deep administrative spatial control arrays.

## 3. Player Movement & Camera Navigation

### 3.1. Navigation Modes
1. **First-Person Exploration (`FIRST_PERSON`)**:
   * Controls: Desktop keyboard `WASD` / Arrow keys for walking, Mouse look with pointer lock.
   * Mobile: Left virtual on-screen joystick for movement, drag right screen area for camera orientation.
   * Collision Detection: Raycast bumper keeping the user inside bounds and preventing clipping through vehicles/walls.
2. **Orbit / Overview (`ORBIT`)**:
   * Allows rotating around vehicles or central rooms using smooth orbital controls with constrained pitch angles.
3. **Focused Inspection (`INSPECTION`)**:
   * Triggered when selecting an object (e.g. clicking a vehicle, terminal, or contract).
   * Camera smoothly interpolates (lerps) along a cubic bezier spline to a predefined inspection vantage point.
4. **Teleportation Portals (`TELEPORT`)**:
   * Walking into a luminous doorway or selecting a waypoint triggers a screen space bloom transition, smoothly repositioning the user to the destination sector.

### 3.2. Camera Director Implementation
* Smooth camera transitions handled by custom damping hooks (`useFrame` with Three.js `MathUtils.damp3` and `dampQ`).
* Respects `prefers-reduced-motion` media queries: when enabled, camera transitions use subtle instant cross-fades rather than high-speed continuous motion.
